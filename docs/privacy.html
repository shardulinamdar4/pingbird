Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Pingbird has no server, no database, no account system, and no telemetry. It is an open-source Google Apps Script that runs entirely inside your own Google account. This page is the complete data-flow map; because the source is public, every claim below is verifiable.

Data we (the developers) collect

None. No email content, no metadata, no analytics, no crash reports. We operate no infrastructure that your data could reach.

Sanitization before AI

Before any text is sent to your AI provider, Pingbird strips URLs, phone numbers, email addresses, and long numeric identifiers, and truncates bodies. Requests are sent with OpenRouter's data_collection: "deny" provider preference (no training on your data); if your chosen model has no endpoint under that policy, Pingbird retries without it. You can pin a specific model for stricter control.

Phone notifications

Alerts are intentionally content-free: no sender, subject, company, role, or deadline. A notification on a lock screen reveals only that something in a spreadsheet needs attention.

Google user data & Limited Use

Pingbird's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the user-facing tracking features described here; it is never used for advertising, never sold, and never transferred except as described in the table above (to the AI provider you configure, at your direction) or as required by law.

Secrets

Your OpenRouter key and Pocket Alert webhook are stored in Google Apps Script User Properties — scoped to your Google account, never displayed in any sheet, and not included when the spreadsheet is copied.

Permissions requested, and why

  • Gmail (modify) — read career email, apply/remove three labels, keep action emails in the Inbox, archive confirmed no-action mail.
  • Spreadsheets — maintain the Tracker and hidden state sheets.
  • External requests — call OpenRouter and your Pocket Alert webhook; these are the only endpoints in the code, enforced by the manifest's URL whitelist.
  • Triggers — run the five daily scans.

Data deletion

Delete the spreadsheet and remove the three Gmail labels to erase all Pingbird data. Revoke the script's access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. There is nothing to delete on our side, because nothing exists on our side.
